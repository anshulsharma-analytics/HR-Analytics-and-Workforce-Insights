# HR Analytics and Workforce Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics and Workforce Insights Dashboard** is a Power BI data analytics project designed to analyze employee demographics, workforce distribution, compensation, job satisfaction, and employee attrition.

The objective of this project is to transform raw HR data into meaningful business insights that help HR teams and management understand workforce trends, identify potential attrition patterns, and support data-driven employee retention strategies.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze overall employee attrition and retention trends.
* Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition.
* Understand the relationship between employee attrition and monthly income.
* Evaluate average working years across departments.
* Analyze work-life balance across different job roles.
* Examine the relationship between attrition and years since the last promotion.
* Provide an interactive dashboard for workforce monitoring and decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

* **Power BI Desktop** — Dashboard development and data visualization.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** — KPI calculations and analytical measures.
* **Power Query** — Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
* **SQL** — Data querying and validation, where applicable.
* **Microsoft Excel** — Source data preparation and preliminary analysis.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following key metrics:

* Total Employees
* Total Employee Attrition
* Attrition Rate
* Average Monthly Income
* Average Hourly Rate
* Average Working Years

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Employee Attrition Analysis

* Overall attrition rate.
* Attrition by department and job role.
* Attrition trends across employee groups.

### 2. Compensation Analysis

* Attrition versus monthly income.
* Average hourly rate analysis.
* Compensation comparisons across workforce categories.

### 3. Workforce Experience Analysis

* Average working years by department.
* Attrition versus years since the last promotion.
* Workforce distribution based on experience.

### 4. Job Satisfaction and Work-Life Balance

* Job role versus work-life balance.
* Analysis of employee satisfaction indicators.
* Identification of workforce segments that may require further investigation.

### 5. Interactive Reporting

* Interactive slicers and filters.
* Cross-filtering between visuals.
* Drill-down analysis across employee categories.
* KPI cards and charts for quick business insights.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Identify and review the HR dataset and its business attributes.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Check missing values, duplicate employee records, data types, and data consistency.
3. **Data Transformation:** Use Power Query to prepare the dataset for analysis.
4. **Data Modeling:** Organize the data model and configure appropriate relationships where required.
5. **DAX Development:** Create measures for employee counts, attrition rate, and workforce metrics.
6. **Dashboard Development:** Build interactive Power BI reports and visualizations.
7. **Data Validation:** Reconcile important KPI calculations with source data.
8. **Insight Generation:** Analyze workforce patterns to support HR decision-making.

## 💡 Business Value

This dashboard demonstrates how business intelligence can help HR teams:

* Monitor workforce composition and attrition.
* Identify employee groups that may need further retention analysis.
* Investigate compensation, work-life balance, and career progression patterns.
* Support workforce planning with data-driven insights.
* Improve the accessibility of HR metrics through interactive reporting.

**Note:** The dashboard highlights associations and patterns in the available data. These patterns do not, by themselves, establish the causes of employee attrition.
